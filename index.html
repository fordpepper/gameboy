Tips

Landscape is the most comfortable. Touches anywhere in a side column work, so you don’t have to hit the pad exactly. Plug in or pair a controller (or use a keyboard) and the screen grows to full size; touch the screen to bring the buttons back. Keyboard: arrows, X = A, Z = B, Enter = Start, Shift = Select.
