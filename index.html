Tips

Landscape is the most comfortable: thumbs rest on the bottom corners. Touches anywhere in the left column steer the D-pad, so you don’t have to hit it exactly. Keyboard: arrows, X = A, Z = B, Enter = Start, Shift = Select. Gamepads work too.
